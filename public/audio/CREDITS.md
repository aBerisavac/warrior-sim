## Original procedural sound design

Bellows, quenching steam, grinding, and the non-vocal dialogue letter tone are generated by this project's Web Audio code in src/audio.js. They are not external recordings. Downloaded clips may be mixed at different levels or with small runtime pitch variations. Edit src/data/audio.json to replace or remap sounds.


## Encounter cues

Bad, good, and neutral encounter stings are original procedural musical cues generated by the game’s Web Audio mixer. They use descending minor tones, an ascending major arpeggio, and a short balanced motif respectively. No external recordings are used for these cues.

## Market ambience

- `market-crowd.mp3`: **Outdoor Market #2**, Joseph SARDIN / BigSoundBank, CC0. Source: https://bigsoundbank.com/outdoor-market-2-s3378.html . Original recording of market stalls and conversation, used quietly beneath game audio.
- `market-watch-out.wav`, `market-coming-through.wav`: original short English callouts rendered locally using Windows System.Speech (Microsoft Zira). No third-party voice samples downloaded.
- Cart wheel rattle: original procedural audio in `src/audio.js`.

## Road footsteps — TinyWorlds

`road-gravel.ogg` and `road-stone.ogg` replace the previous Kenney grass/impact footsteps. They are unchanged recordings `gravel.ogg` and `stone01.ogg` from **Different steps on wood, stone, leaves, gravel and mud**, TinyWorlds, CC0.
Source and licence: https://opengameart.org/content/different-steps-on-wood-stone-leaves-gravel-and-mud
Archive: https://opengameart.org/sites/default/files/%5Bkdd%5DDifferentSteps_0.zip
Playback uses lower gain, slight pitch/volume variation, and no immediate repeat. The previous grass recordings remain as unused assets.

## Continuous road walking

`road-walking-loop.mp3`: **Footsteps on Gravels #4**, Joseph SARDIN & Axeline T., BigSoundBank, CC0. Outdoor first-person footsteps recorded along a mountain path.
Source/licence: https://bigsoundbank.com/footsteps-on-gravels-4-s3216.html
Download: https://bigsoundbank.com/UPLOAD/mp3/3216.mp3
The decoded recording has a short overlap at the loop seam. Playback fades with movement and smoothly varies speed and volume over 2–4-second intervals. Replaces the individual road-footstep samples above, which remain unused assets.
