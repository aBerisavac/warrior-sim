# Game writing

- Write activity introductions and the protagonist's reflections as calm, natural first-person inner thoughts, as though he is considering what to do next.
- Keep NPC speech in the speaker's own voice. Keep factual UI labels, statistics, objectives, and map information clear; they do not need to be inner thoughts.
- Put exact mechanical explanations outside narrative thoughts where practical. Keep implementation notes and placeholder-development explanations in project documentation rather than character dialogue.
- Refer to the protagonist's home naturally as home or Father's house by the forge, while giving players enough context to identify the blacksmith location.
- Store all player-facing copy in the existing i18n catalogs.

- Dialogue typography: put spoken words in typographic quotation marks and inner thoughts in italics without quotes. Split mixed speech and thought into separate localized segments, preserving the speaker portrait. Keep factual narration and posted notices unquoted.
